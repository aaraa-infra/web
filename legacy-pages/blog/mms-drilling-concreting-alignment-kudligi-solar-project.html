Project Overview

AARAA Infrastructure undertook survey marking, MMS drilling, concreting and alignment works for a solar project at Kudligi, Karnataka.

The documented work package covers Block II — 20 MWp and focuses on the accurate preparation and execution of the infrastructure required for the Module Mounting Structure (MMS) installation.

The project demonstrates AARAA Infrastructure's capabilities in executing specialized solar civil and site-development works, where survey accuracy, foundation positioning, drilling, concreting and structural alignment are critical to subsequent solar-module installation.

Solar projects require precise coordination between surveying, civil works and Module Mounting Structure installation. At the Kudligi project, AARAA Infrastructure's documented work package covered the preparation and execution of MMS-related civil works for Block II — 20 MWp.

The work involved establishing accurate locations through survey marking, carrying out drilling and concreting activities and ensuring appropriate alignment for the subsequent installation process.

Survey Marking

Accurate survey marking is a fundamental stage of solar-site execution. Before drilling and concreting activities begin, the required locations need to be established according to the project layout.

The documented scope included survey marking, providing the positioning reference for the subsequent MMS-related works.

This stage is particularly important because inaccuracies in positioning can affect the installation and alignment of downstream structural components.

MMS Drilling

The project scope included MMS drilling for the solar installation. Module Mounting Structures require accurately positioned foundations or supports to accommodate the solar-module mounting system.

AARAA Infrastructure's work package involved carrying out the required drilling activities in accordance with the designated project locations and execution requirements.

Concreting Works

Following the drilling activities, the scope included concreting works associated with the MMS infrastructure. Concrete works form an important part of establishing the required support infrastructure for the mounting system.

The execution required coordination between:

Survey → Drilling → Concreting → Alignment

to maintain consistency across the installation area.

MMS Alignment

Alignment is a critical component of Module Mounting Structure preparation. Accurate positioning and alignment help ensure that the completed foundation/support system is ready for subsequent MMS installation and solar-module deployment.

The Kudligi work package therefore combined the preceding civil activities with MMS alignment, creating an integrated execution sequence rather than treating drilling and concreting as isolated activities.

Integrated Solar Civil Execution Sequence

The project demonstrates the importance of coordinating multiple activities during solar-site construction. The documented sequence can be represented as:

Survey Marking → MMS Drilling → Concreting → Alignment → MMS Installation Readiness

This integrated approach helps maintain consistency across the solar project site.
