RCC Precast Drain Construction

RCC precast drainage systems provide a structured solution for managing stormwater within developed sites.

For this project, the work focused on the systematic installation of precast drain units after preparing and levelling the supporting base. Proper positioning and joint sealing were undertaken to create a continuous drainage pathway. The execution approach helped maintain the required continuity of the drain while supporting efficient stormwater movement.

Site Preparation & Base Levelling

Proper preparation of the drainage alignment is an important part of precast drain installation.

The project included:

  • Preparing the work area
  • Establishing the drainage alignment
  • Base preparation
  • Levelling the supporting surface
  • Preparing the location for placement of precast units

This provided a suitable foundation for the subsequent installation activities.

Precise Placement of Precast Units

The RCC precast drain units were placed systematically along the prepared alignment.

Precise placement helps maintain continuity throughout the drainage system and ensures that individual precast sections work together as a connected drainage channel. For the project, this installation was carried out across a total length of 285 meters.

Joint Sealing & Drainage Continuity

Joint sealing formed part of the project execution to support continuity between the installed precast drain sections.

Properly sealed joints contribute to the overall integrity of the drainage installation while helping maintain efficient water-flow conditions along the completed drain.
