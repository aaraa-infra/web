AARAA Infrastructure completed External Development Works for SRM Group at Kattankulathur, Chennai, delivering a comprehensive package of campus infrastructure and external civil works.

Completed in 2025 with a project value of ₹3.5 Crore, the project involved the development of external infrastructure including subgrade preparation, GSB and WMM works, RCC drainage, paver-block paving, granite flooring, landscaping, compound-wall works, and lift-related works.

Detailed Scope of Work

  • Subgrade Preparation: Compaction and formation levelling establishing a rigid foundation for campus traffic.
  • GSB & WMM Layers: Granular Sub-Base (GSB) and Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) engineered road-base construction.
  • RCC Drain Construction: Reinforced concrete storm water drainage network preventing campus waterlogging.
  • Paver Block & Granite Flooring: Heavy-duty interlocking paver block driveways and polished granite pedestrian walkways.
  • Landscaping & Compound Walls: Perimeter security compound wall construction and softscape landscape integration.
  • Lift Works: Structural civil works and elevator shaft infrastructure installation.
