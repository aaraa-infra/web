Civil Construction Works

Civil execution formed an essential foundation for the Manapakkam project. The work involved construction-related activities associated with the school's building infrastructure, paying close attention to proper execution of both structural and architectural components within assigned timelines.

AARAA's execution approach focused on coordinating various civil activities in tight sequence—moving efficiently from foundational building works through to structural masonry, wall plastering, and interior completion.

Flooring & Architectural Finishing Works

High-durability flooring and architectural finishes are critical for high-footfall educational environments. The project included complete internal surface preparation and finishing activities so that completed classrooms, hallways, and administrative areas were fully prepared for academic use:

  • Sub-Base & Floor Preparation: Level concrete base preparation ensuring smooth tile alignment and damp-proofing.
  • High-Traffic Vitrified Flooring: Installation of heavy-duty vitrified floor tiles across classrooms and corridors.
  • Wall Surface Plastering & Painting: Smooth internal wall plastering topped with washable, anti-bacterial emulsion paint.
  • Doors & Windows: Precision fixing of durable flush doors, hardware fittings, and safety-engineered window frames.

Institutional Building Works & Service Integration

Constructing for educational clients requires seamless coordination between structural, architectural, and service-related trades. For the Manapakkam campus, AARAA Infrastructure's role involved executing the defined civil and building works while aligning sanitary, drainage, and plumbing infrastructure.

The primary objective was to deliver functional, aesthetic, and hygienic learning spaces tailored precisely to the operational standards of K12 Techno Services.

Educational Infrastructure Execution Standards

Executing school infrastructure demands strict operational discipline across key project parameters:

  • ✔️ Construction Quality: Compliance with IS structural codes and institutional specifications.
  • ✔️ Finishing Standards: Durable, child-safe, non-toxic wall paints and anti-skid floor surfaces.
  • ✔️ Material Handling: Staged material storage to preserve campus cleanliness and safety.
  • ✔️ Multi-Trade Sequencing: Integrated coordination between civil, architectural, and plumbing teams.
