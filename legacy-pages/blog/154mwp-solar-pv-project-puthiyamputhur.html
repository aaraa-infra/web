Project Overview

AARAA Infrastructure's renewable-energy project portfolio includes civil and infrastructure work associated with a 154 MWp Solar PV Project at Puthiyamputhur, Tamil Nadu.

The project documentation identifies a large-scale solar photovoltaic development with a 154 MWp DC capacity and 90.625 MW AC capacity, located at Puthiyamputhur.

The project represents AARAA Infrastructure's experience in supporting large-scale renewable-energy developments through project-specific civil and infrastructure execution.

Large-scale photovoltaic projects require extensive supporting infrastructure to prepare the site for solar generation equipment and associated facilities. The 154 MWp Puthiyamputhur Solar PV Project forms part of AARAA Infrastructure's renewable-energy project experience, demonstrating the company's involvement in infrastructure requirements associated with utility-scale solar developments.

The project combines a substantial DC solar-generation capacity with an associated 90.625 MW AC capacity, representing a significant renewable-energy development in Tamil Nadu.
