Project Overview

AARAA Infrastructure undertook civil and renovation-related works for Orchids International School at Vandalur, Chennai, as part of its ongoing educational infrastructure portfolio.

The documented Vandalur work package included dismantling and removal activities associated with the existing school facility, together with related civil works required for the site.

The project demonstrates AARAA Infrastructure's experience in executing controlled civil and renovation works within existing institutional environments.

Renovation and modification of an existing educational facility require careful handling of existing structures and controlled execution. At the Orchids International School — Vandalur project, AARAA Infrastructure carried out the documented work package with attention to:

  • Existing-structure conditions
  • Controlled dismantling
  • Removal of identified building elements
  • Site safety
  • Material handling
  • Debris management
  • Coordination with ongoing site activities
  • Preparation of areas for subsequent works

Dismantling & Removal Works

The Vandalur work documentation includes dismantling-related activities involving existing building components. The scope included activities such as:

  • Dismantling of existing doors
  • Dismantling of existing brickwork
  • Dismantling of concrete elements
  • Shifting and removal activities
  • Disposal of dismantled materials
  • Associated site-clearance activities

Such works require controlled execution to avoid unnecessary impact on surrounding structures and facilities.

Controlled Renovation Execution

Dismantling is often an important first stage in institutional renovation. AARAA Infrastructure approaches such work with emphasis on:

Assessment → Controlled Dismantling → Removal → Site Clearance → Preparation for Subsequent Works

This helps ensure that the designated areas are safely prepared for the next phase of construction or renovation.
