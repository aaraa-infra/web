AARAA Infrastructure undertook geotechnical investigation and pile testing works for a solar project at Kudligi, Karnataka, supporting the technical design and foundation preparation.

The documented work package included pull-out tests, compression tests, and lateral tests, together with associated drilling, testing, and soil investigation activities.

Pile Testing Methodology

⬆️ Pull-Out Testing

Evaluates pile resistance against upward uplift forces generated by high wind speeds on solar arrays.

⬇️ Compression Testing

Measures foundation settlement and load-bearing capacity under downward structural loads.

↔️ Lateral Testing

Assesses horizontal displacement and shear resistance under lateral wind shear forces.
