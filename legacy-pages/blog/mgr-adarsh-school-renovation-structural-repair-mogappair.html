Comprehensive Project Scope

The scope of work delivered by AARAA Infrastructure encompassed complete civil renovation, structural integrity enhancement, and facade restoration:

  • Main Entrance Structure Renovation & Upgrade: Refurbishment and structural enhancement of the primary school entrance, creating a welcoming, safe, and visually commanding entry archway.
  • Civil Repair & Structural Strengthening: Repairing distressed structural components, reinforcing load-bearing columns, and restoring deteriorated concrete surfaces.
  • Plastering, Surface Restoration & Crack Treatment: Application of high-durability polymer-modified mortars, replastering damaged walls, and sealing structural cracks to prevent moisture ingress.
  • Masonry & Boundary Wall Enhancement: Restoration of perimeter masonry walls and boundary enclosures to upgrade campus security and perimeter integrity.
  • Architectural Finishing & Painting: Exterior surface preparation, protective weather-shield coating, and architectural paint finishing matching institutional aesthetics.
  • Operational Campus Site Coordination: Implementation of dedicated safety barriers, dust suppression, and phased construction windows tailored to school timings.

Institutional Infrastructure & Main Entrance Renovation

Educational institutions demand a combination of functional efficiency, aesthetic appeal, and uncompromised safety. At MGR Adarsh School, Mogappair, the main entrance serves as both a primary security access point and the visual identity of the institution.

AARAA Infrastructure redesigned and renovated the entrance zone to improve pedestrian flow, enhance vehicular access safety during peak pick-up/drop-off hours, and reinforce the structural framing of the entrance gateway. The masonry and structural elements were treated with modern weather-resistant finishes to ensure long-term durability against Chennai's coastal climate.

Structural Repair & Specialized Civil Works

Over time, exposure to environmental factors can cause spalling, superficial micro-cracking, and moisture infiltration in civil structures. AARAA Infrastructure's engineering team executed systematically planned structural repairs across affected zones of the school infrastructure:

Concrete Rehabilitation

Removal of loose concrete, anti-corrosion coating for exposed rebar, and application of structural repair mortars for full load restoration.

Moisture Protection

Comprehensive waterproofing, joint sealing, and coping treatments on exposed parapets and entrance structures to eliminate water seepage.

Execution Approach in Active Academic Environments

Working within an active school environment required an uncompromising commitment to safety. AARAA Infrastructure established strict zero-harm protocols:

  • Phased Non-Peak Execution: Heavy structural noise-generating activities were scheduled outside school operating hours and during weekend windows.
  • Barricading & Safety Zones: Heavy-duty physical barricades separated active construction zones from student walkways and play areas.
  • Quality Control & Supervision: Continuous site supervision ensured adherence to IS code civil standards and client specifications.
