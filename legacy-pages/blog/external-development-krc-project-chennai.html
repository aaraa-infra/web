AARAA Infrastructure executed external development and civil infrastructure works for the K Raheja Corp KRC Project in Chennai.

The project involved a broad range of civil and external development activities, including excavation and earthwork, foundation and structural works, site levelling, utility and service line works, retaining walls, boundary works, roads, paving, drainage and stormwater management.

With a project value of ₹3.2 Crore, the work represents a significant civil infrastructure package within the KRC Project.

About the Project

The KRC Project required coordinated civil and external development works to support the site's overall infrastructure.

AARAA Infrastructure's scope covered both foundational civil activities and external site development, extending from earthwork and structural works to roads, paving, utilities and drainage infrastructure.

The project was executed with emphasis on coordinated site development, quality assurance and timely delivery.

Scope of Work

The major scope of work executed by AARAA Infrastructure included:

01. Excavation and Earthwork

Excavation and earthwork formed part of the project's initial civil development activities. These works supported the preparation and development of designated areas across the project site.

02. Foundation and Structural Works

The project included foundation and structural works as part of the overall civil construction package. These activities contributed to the development of the required structural infrastructure within the project.

03. External Development & Site Levelling

External development and site levelling works were undertaken to prepare the surrounding areas and establish appropriate site conditions for subsequent infrastructure works.

04. Utility & Service Line Works

The scope also included utility and service line works, supporting the project's external infrastructure requirements.

05. Retaining Walls & Boundary Works

Retaining wall and boundary-related works formed an important part of the external development package, contributing to site definition and supporting infrastructure.

06. Roads & Paving Works

Road and paving works were included within the project scope to develop functional movement and access areas across the site.

07. Drainage & Stormwater Management

The project also incorporated drainage and stormwater management works, supporting effective management of surface water within the developed site.
