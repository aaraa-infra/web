RCC Road Construction Methodology

The documented road construction scope follows a structured civil execution sequence:

1. Earthwork Excavation

The first stage involves preparing the road formation through the required excavation and earthwork operations. Proper excavation establishes the required formation level for the subsequent pavement layers.

2. Subgrade Preparation

The prepared formation is developed into a suitable subgrade for the road structure. Subgrade preparation is important for establishing a stable base for the pavement system.

3. Granular Sub-Base

The road construction includes provision of a granular sub-base, forming an important structural layer beneath the upper pavement system.

4. Wet Mix Macadam

The documented scope includes WMM — Wet Mix Macadam, providing another engineered layer within the road pavement structure.

5. Lean Concrete

A lean concrete layer forms part of the documented road construction sequence, providing the required base/interface for the subsequent concrete pavement.

6. RCC Concrete Pavement

The final road structure includes RCC concrete pavement, providing the finished hard surface required for the industrial road.
