AARAA Infrastructure successfully delivered comprehensive infrastructure development works executed across multiple premier campuses of Orchid International School in Chennai, including Pallikaranai, Velachery, and Manapakkam.

The multi-campus project encompassed full-spectrum civil engineering, interior architectural fit-outs, high-durability sports facilities, pre-engineered buildings (PEB), sewage treatment plant (STP) setup, and campus utility infrastructure—designed to create an inspiring, future-ready learning environment for students and faculty.

With a contract value of ₹3.21 Crore spanning over 12,525 sq.ft of specialized construction, AARAA Infrastructure handled multi-trade synchronization under aggressive delivery schedules to guarantee uninterrupted academic operations.

Civil & Structural Engineering

Institutional construction demands superior structural durability and low lifecycle maintenance. AARAA Infrastructure deployed specialized RCC engineering, precision masonry, and pre-engineered structural steel to ensure longevity under continuous daily footfall:

Structural Engineering

  • Engineered sub-base leveling and RCC foundations
  • High-strength AAC block and solid brick masonry
  • Crack-resistant cement plastering with bonding agents
  • Multi-layer elastomer waterproofing for basements and terraces

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB)

  • High-clearance PEB structural frameworks
  • Insulated roofing sheets for thermal and acoustic regulation
  • Rapid modular installation to meet academic deadlines
  • Corrosion-resistant protective coatings

Professional Sports Arena & Outdoor Amenities

A major highlight of the project was creating world-class sports facilities compliant with educational athletic safety codes:

  • FIBA-Standard Basketball Courts: High-grade asphalt/concrete base with multi-coat UV-stable acrylic surfacing, official regulation markings, and heavy-duty rebound netting.
  • All-Weather Futsal Arenas: Shock-absorbent synthetic turf with silica sand and SBR rubber infill, surrounding perimeter fencing, and glare-free floodlighting setup.
  • Skating Rink & Play Zones: Precision micro-topped smooth polyurethane surface designed for anti-slip safety, complete with shock-absorbing safety crash rails.
  • Heavy-Duty Driveway Paving: 80mm heavy-traffic interlocking concrete pavers laid over compacted WMM/GSB sub-bases for bus circulation and parent pickup zones.
