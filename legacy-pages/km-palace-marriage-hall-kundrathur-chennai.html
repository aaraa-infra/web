Engineering Excellence for Large-Scale Commercial Venues

AARAA Infrastructure Private Limited successfully engineered and constructed KM Palace (Royal Marriage Hall & Convention Centre), located at Sirukalathur Main Road, Chembarambakkam, near Kundrathur Outer Ring Road, Chennai. Delivered at a total turnkey contract value of ₹7.74 Crore, the facility spans 12,000 Sq.Ft of high-capacity commercial built-up area.

Designed from a general contractor's perspective to handle extreme load conditions, high guest traffic, and complex architectural acoustics, AARAA Infrastructure managed the full construction lifecycle—from soil stabilization and heavy raft foundation casting to long-span Post-Tensioned (PT) concrete slabs, Pre-Engineered Steel Roofing, central HVAC, bio-STP filtration, and luxury interior joinery.

Civil & Structural Engineering Scope

Constructing a large-capacity public gathering venue required rigorous structural calculation and structural steel integration to ensure complete safety and durability:

  • Post-Tensioned (PT) Concrete Slabs: Engineered long-span PT beam & slab systems to eliminate interior structural columns, creating a completely pillarless main hall accommodating 2,500+ floating guests.
  • Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) Roof Trusses: High-tensile steel PEB roof framework with insulated standing seam roofing sheets providing acoustic damping during monsoon seasons.
  • Heavy Load Foundations: Reinforced RCC raft foundations engineered for high-salinity water tables near the Chembarambakkam reservoir area.
  • Anti-Corrosive Concrete Mix: M35/M40 grade concrete with micro-silica additives to prevent chemical degradation and rebar corrosion.

Integrated MEP, HVAC & Utility Infrastructure

To guarantee seamless operations during high-capacity wedding celebrations, AARAA Infrastructure designed and commissioned complete electro-mechanical systems:

  • HVAC & Air Conditioning: High-efficiency VRF / Chilled Water HVAC system delivering uniform cooling across 12,000 Sq.Ft of public & dining areas.
  • 100% Diesel Generator Power Backup: Heavy-duty commercial DG sets connected to an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) for zero-downtime power transfer.
  • Bio Sewage Treatment Plant (STP): Environmentally compliant commercial Bio-STP for recycling kitchen & washroom wastewater for landscaping and toilet flushing.
  • Fire Fighting & Safety: Automated sprinkler systems, hose reels, fire pumps, smoke detectors, and emergency escape stairwells adhering to NBC standards.
  • Elevator & Vertical Mobility: Installation and load-testing of commercial passenger elevators serving multi-level guest suites and dining floors.
