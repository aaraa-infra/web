Images displayed in the AARAA Infrastructure Gallery and project pages are intended to represent AARAA Infrastructure's project portfolio, construction activities, infrastructure works and related corporate activities.

Gallery photographs may include:

  • Completed projects
  • Projects under execution
  • Construction activities
  • Site photographs

Images may be permanently watermarked or otherwise protected to identify their association with AARAA Infrastructure.

4. Prohibited Image Usage

Unless written permission has been obtained from AARAA Infrastructure, users must not:

  • Copy or reproduce Gallery photographs for commercial purposes.
  • Remove, obscure or alter watermarks.
  • Crop an image specifically to remove a watermark.
  • Re-upload AARAA images to another website.
  • Use AARAA project photographs to represent another company or contractor's work.
  • Use project photographs in advertisements.
  • Use images in brochures, presentations or marketing materials.
  • Sell, license or redistribute the images.
  • Use the images to create derivative promotional materials.
  • Claim ownership of AARAA project photographs.
  • Use the photographs in a misleading or deceptive manner.
  • Use photographs to imply an affiliation, partnership or endorsement that does not exist.

5. Personal & Non-Commercial Reference

Limited use of website content for genuine personal or informational reference may be permitted where it does not infringe copyright or misrepresent AARAA Infrastructure.

Where permission is granted, appropriate attribution may be required.

Permission to view content on the website does not transfer ownership or copyright.

Project names, client names, locations and other project references may be used for portfolio, corporate communication and informational purposes where AARAA Infrastructure is authorised to publish such information.

Nothing on this website grants a licence to use any trademark, logo or brand identity.

9. Request for Permission

If you wish to use an AARAA Infrastructure photograph, project image, written content, logo or other copyrighted material, please contact us with:

  • Your name or organisation

  • Where the material will be published
  • Expected duration of use

Permission must be obtained before using protected content.

10. Copyright Infringement

If you believe that content appearing on the website infringes your copyright or other intellectual property rights, please contact AARAA Infrastructure with sufficient information to identify:

AARAA Infrastructure may review legitimate complaints and take appropriate action.
