Engineering the Civil Backbone of Renewable Energy in Tamil Nadu

A solar power generation facility is only as durable as the ground and civil substructures supporting it. While solar modules and inverters are factory-manufactured electrical components, site civil infrastructure must endure decades of high wind uplift, seismic stresses, coastal soil salinity, and severe monsoon storm runoff.

Headquartered in Chennai, AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. operates as a specialized Civil Balance of Plant (Civil-BoP) Contractor. We partner directly with utility-scale solar power developers, Independent Power Producers (IPPs), and EPC main contractors to deliver self-executed, heavy civil packages that safeguard generation assets from structural misalignment, differential settlement, and perimeter erosion.

From the black-cotton soils and coastal salt-marsh corridors of Thoothukudi and Ramanathapuram to the hard-rock terrains of Tirunelveli and Karur, AARAA brings calibrated plant machinery, licensed survey instruments, and experienced site engineers who understand Tamil Nadu's complex regional geography.

Geotechnical Rigour

Civil Engineering & Substructure Foundation Works

Tamil Nadu's diverse solar development regions present contrasting geotechnical conditions. In southern coastal belts (Thoothukudi, Tirunelveli), expansive marine clays and high water tables require deep pile friction designs and sulfate-resistant concreting. In central corridors (Dindigul, Karur, Tiruppur), shallow weathered rock and boulder beds demand rotary diamond core drilling and rock socketing.

AARAA Infrastructure approaches every foundation package with rigorous engineering controls:

Parameter AARAA Engineering Specification Field Quality Control
MMS Piling Type Bored cast-in-situ concrete piles (typically 250mm–350mm dia, 1.8m–3.5m depth depending on soil strata). Auger depth measurement, bore cleaning verification, tremie pipe concrete pouring.
Concrete Mix Design M25 to M35 design mix with water-cement ratio ≤ 0.45; Fly ash / GGBS blend for coastal durability. Batching plant calibration, slump testing, 7-day & 28-day cube strength testing at accredited labs.
Setting Out Tolerances Center-to-center pile spacing ±5mm; vertical plumbness ≤ 1:100; stub top elevation ±3mm. Dual-frequency DGPS and digital total stations used for master grid alignment.
Load Testing (IS 2911) Initial and routine testing for vertical compression, horizontal shear, and vertical pullout (uplift). Calibrated hydraulic jacks, load cells, and dial gauge deflection tracking.

Subgrade & Drainage Engineering

Uncontrolled surface water runoff is the leading cause of soil erosion, pile exposure, and road washouts on utility solar sites. We engineer site-wide surface water management:

Engineered Earthen & Masonry Drains
Trapezoidal earthen boundary drains and stone-pitched chutes sized according to 25-year return period rainfall intensity calculations for the specific Tamil Nadu catchment zone.
Reinforced Pipe Culverts
NP3/NP4 class hume pipe culverts installed with headwalls and wingwalls at every arterial road crossing to prevent access interruption during peak monsoons.
Proactive Subgrade Compaction
Subgrade compacted to minimum 95% Modified Proctor Density (IS 2720 Part 8) verified with field sand replacement and core cutter testing prior to road construction.

HSE & Quality Governance

Rigorous Quality Control & Zero-Harm Safety Standards

Utility solar sites involve high-risk construction activities: heavy moving earthmoving equipment, overhead high-voltage lines, open excavations, and extreme ambient heat. AARAA enforces uncompromising safety and material quality regimes across all work fronts.

Quality Control Protocols:

  • Concrete Strength Verification: Regular 150mm concrete test cubes cast for every batch and tested for 7-day and 28-day compressive strength at certified independent laboratories.
  • Subgrade Compaction Density: In-situ field dry density tested using core-cutter and sand-replacement methods to guarantee ≥95% compaction.
  • Steel Rebar Validation: Mill Test Certificates (MTC) verified for yield strength, elongation, and chemical composition for every rebar consignment.

Health, Safety & Environment (HSE):

  • Mandatory Daily Toolbox Talks (TBT) before every work shift
  • 100% Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) compliance (helmets, high-vis vests, steel-toe boots)
  • Documented practical emergency preparedness drills and CPR awareness
  • Heat stress hydration stations and dedicated first-aid response posts

Procurement & Technical Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions — Solar Infrastructure in Tamil Nadu

Detailed technical and commercial answers for project owners, procurement heads, and EPC managers.

What is the scope of Civil Balance of Plant (Civil BoP) for solar projects handled by AARAA?

AARAA Infrastructure's Civil BoP scope covers all non-electrical civil packages required to prepare a site for module mounting and grid interconnection. This includes digital topographical surveys, bulk earthworks, site grading, pile drilling and cast-in-situ concrete foundations for Module Mounting Structures (MMS), heavy Inverter Control Room (ICR) buildings, transformer plinths and oil soak pits, engineered peripheral and internal arterial access roads, stormwater drainage culverts, and precast security boundary wall systems.

What verified utility-scale solar project experience does AARAA have in Tamil Nadu?

AARAA executed major civil infrastructure packages for WAAREE Renewable Technologies Limited on the 180 MWp Leap Green Solar Project located at Poovani/Maniyachi in Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu. The consolidated basic order value was approximately ₹7.64 Crore, delivering 33.47 km of total site roads (28.47 km periphery road + 5 km internal roads), 5 km of precast boundary fencing with Y-angle barbed wire, and reinforced concrete foundation packages for Inverter Control Rooms (ICR).

How does AARAA handle coastal corrosion in Tamil Nadu solar sites like Thoothukudi and Ennore?

In coastal belts where soil and groundwater exhibit high chloride and sulfate salinity, AARAA protects foundation durability by specifying Sulfate Resisting Portland Cement (SRPC) or fly-ash blended Portland Pozzolana Cement (PPC), epoxy-coated or corrosion-resistant rebar, increased concrete clear cover (minimum 50-75 mm), and bitumen mastic coatings on underground concrete surfaces, validated by pre-construction geotechnical soil bore-hole analysis.

What pile testing procedures are conducted prior to full-scale MMS installation?

Prior to mass piling, AARAA conducts initial pile load testing across representative project blocks in accordance with IS 2911 (Part 4). This includes vertical compressive load testing, lateral load testing, and vertical pullout (uplift) testing to determine ultimate uplift capacity against extreme coastal wind gusts. Pile coordinates and levels are established using calibrated digital total stations.

Can AARAA Infrastructure execute civil works while another contractor handles module mounting and electrical cabling?

Yes. AARAA frequently collaborates with Tier-1 solar EPC contractors, developers, and IPPs as the specialized Civil Infrastructure Subcontractor. We handle all civil earthworks, substructures, roads, and buildings, while coordinating milestone handovers directly with electrical balance of plant (E-BoP) and module installation teams.

What specifications are followed for internal and periphery solar plant roads?

Periphery security roads are typically constructed at 2.5 m width with earthen or gravel shoulders, while internal access roads are 3.0 m to 4.5 m wide to accommodate 40-tonne transport trailers and mobile cranes carrying power transformers and inverters. Road designs incorporate granular sub-base (GSB), wet mix macadam (WMM), camber gradients for rainwater runoff, and reinforced pipe culverts at natural drainage crossings.

Does AARAA Infrastructure undertake industrial rooftop solar structural reinforcements in Chennai?

Yes. For manufacturing plants, warehouses, and commercial IT complexes in industrial hubs like Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Ambattur, and Guindy, AARAA conducts structural load assessments on existing PEB rafters and RCC slabs, installs structural stub pedestals, non-penetrating ballasted mounts, and walkways engineered to withstand cyclone wind speeds.

How does AARAA mobilize plant and machinery for remote solar plant sites?

AARAA operates its own fleet of heavy earthmoving machinery, transit mixers, mobile batching plants, and hydraulic drilling rigs. We mobilize site teams and equipment directly from our Chennai and regional depots, establishing on-site site offices, equipment yards, and testing laboratories within 10 to 14 days of contract award.
